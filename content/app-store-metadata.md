## Description (4000 characters max)
Stay aware of how much of the month has passed with MonthBar, a minimal macOS MenuBar app that shows your monthly progress at a glance.

**Simple Month Tracking**
MonthBar displays the current month's progress right in your MenuBar. See at a glance how many days have elapsed or remain in the month.

**Two Display Styles**
Choose between:
- **Pie Chart**: Visual arc showing your month's progress
- **Percentage Text**: At-a-glance numeric display

**Customizable Display**
Toggle between viewing:
- **Remaining time**: See how much of the month is left
- **Elapsed time**: See how much of the month has passed

**Privacy-First Design**
MonthBar is a lightweight app that respects your privacy:
- **No network connection** - All calculations performed locally
- **No data collection** - Zero analytics or tracking
- **No accounts required** - Just install and use
- **Settings stay local** - Preferences stored only on your device

**Perfect For**
- Monthly goal tracking
- Budget period awareness
- Project deadline visualization
- Time management awareness
- Habit formation
- Monthly planning

**Features**
- Live MenuBar icon updates automatically
- Two icon styles (Pie Chart, Percentage Text)
- Toggle between remaining and elapsed time
- Full Light and Dark mode support
- Respects your system accent color
- VoiceOver accessibility support
- Minimal system resources
- Native macOS app built with SwiftUI

**Simple and Focused**
MonthBar does one thing well: shows you where you are in the current month. No complexity, no distractions, no feature bloat. Just clean, simple month progress tracking.
