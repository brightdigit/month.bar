# Privacy Policy for MonthBar

**Effective Date**: February 10, 2026
**Last Updated**: February 10, 2026

## Overview

MonthBar is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes what data the app collects and how it is used.

## Data Collection

MonthBar does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data outside of your device. The app does not use:
- Analytics services
- Crash reporting services
- Advertising networks
- Third-party tracking tools

## Local Data Storage

The app stores your preferences locally using macOS UserDefaults:
- Icon style preference (Pie Chart or Percentage Text)
- Progress display preference (Remaining or Elapsed)

This data:
- Remains on your device only
- Is never transmitted over the network
- Is not accessible to the developer
- Can be reset by uninstalling the app

## Network Usage

MonthBar does NOT connect to the internet. All month progress calculations are performed locally on your device using macOS system date and time.

## Third-Party Services

The app does NOT integrate with any third-party services.

## Children's Privacy

MonthBar does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated policy will be posted at this URL with a new "Last Updated" date.

For questions about this privacy policy or the app, please visit our [Support page](/support).

## Consent

By using MonthBar, you consent to this privacy policy.
